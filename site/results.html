The two rows that matter.

contradicted_f1 — the fixture the project exists for. The abstract states "Method B reaches an F1 score of 91.0 on MNLI" verbatim, so evidence_presence passes. The results table reports 86.0, so table_value fails. Both are correct, and a system that collapsed them into one verdict would destroy the only interesting fact about the claim: the paper contradicts its own table.

synthetic_data_passage — the check admitting it cannot decide. Gold says supported, and the passage really does support the claim. But "the model is trained only on synthetic data" is asserted by "no real corpora were used at any stage of training" — a paraphrase. Token coverage is 40%: trained ≠ training, and only ≠ no real corpora were used. A bag-of-words rule cannot bridge that, so the check returns inconclusive, not fail. This is the honest cost of a transparent method, and it is the number to watch: a stronger evidence checker is measured by whether it moves this claim to pass without moving contradicted_f1's passage check off pass.

Same locator, opposite verdict — only the check's recorded inputs tell them apart:

How to read this. A consistency check, not a benchmark. The locator conventions and the table row rule were written with the two committed table fixtures in view. 14/14 means "the linker reproduces the hand-authored provenance exactly, and invents nothing" — a real property, and the one Milestone 5 depends on — not that retrieval is solved. synthetic_data_passage is the only fixture where keyword overlap is load-bearing: the claim appears nowhere verbatim, so the linker must separate a paraphrasing passage (0.211, on the two shared tokens synthetic and data) from a compute-hardware distractor (0.000). It does — but 0.211 is a low absolute score, and the margin exists only because the distractor shares no vocabulary at all. A harder distractor would not be separated by a bag-of-words baseline.

How to read this. Seven hand-authored fixtures are a sanity check, not a benchmark, and the extractor's lexicons were chosen with them in view.

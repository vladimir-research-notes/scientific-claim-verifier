How to read this. Six hand-authored fixtures are a sanity check, not a benchmark, and the extractor's lexicons were chosen with them in view. A 6/6 on datasets means "both dataset mentions present were found" — not that dataset extraction is solved. The 5/6 on conditions is the most informative number here: the miss is synthetic_data_passage, whose gold condition "synthetic data only" is a semantic paraphrase. The extractor returns [] rather than guessing — by design, since an empty field is scored underspecified downstream while a confidently wrong field is not.

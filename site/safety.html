Safety

This project is deliberately scoped to safe, computational, open/synthetic scientific examples. The boundaries below are permanent non-goals, not temporary gaps.

In scope

  • Machine-learning benchmark claims (accuracy/F1 deltas, ablation tables).
  • Bioinformatics toy analyses over synthetic/open data.
  • Statistical claims over small committed CSVs.
  • Simple ablation / results tables.
  • Reproduction of tiny Python scripts computing a reported metric.

Out of scope — always

  • No clinical decision-making and no patient-specific advice.
  • No medical or treatment recommendations.
  • No wet-lab protocol design or optimization.
  • No dual-use biological instructions.
  • No real patient data and no private/real papers.
  • No real credential handling.

Data policy

All fixtures are synthetic or open and small. Secrets, credentials, private papers, large datasets, model weights, and paid-API outputs are never committed (enforced by .gitignore and a repo-hygiene check). See Artifact bundles.

Auditability, not authority

The system's purpose is to make claim-checking transparent and inspectable: it separates the extracted claim, the supporting text, the computed check, the inference, and the unverifiable parts. It does not present itself as an authority on whether a scientific claim is true — only on what the supplied artifacts do or do not support. See Limitations.

Roadmap

Directions beyond the initial milestones — deliberately modest and honest. Nothing here requires paid APIs or GPUs.

Integration contracts (Milestone 11)

Rather than depending on sibling projects, this repo defines compatible import/export formats so it can plug into them later:

code-agent runtime

Consume a verification task as a runnable task; export traces in a compatible JSONL shape.

BioScientist OS

Accept artifact bundles and emit verification results as structured evidence records.

BioAgentBench

Expose the smoke suite as a benchmark with per-task scoring compatible with a harness.

LLM regression / observability

Emit per-run metrics and labels for regression comparison across versions.

agent-control-bench

Provide claim-verification episodes as controllable, replayable units.

Method directions

  • Stronger, still-transparent extraction (grammar/pattern libraries) with the rule-based baseline kept as the interpretable floor.
  • More check types: distributional comparisons, seed-sensitivity checks, and simple figure-value extraction from committed series.
  • Richer failure-mode taxonomy driven by what the smoke suite actually surfaces.
  • Optional, cost-guarded LLM adapters for extraction assistance — with the rule-based path always available and always the default.

What will stay out

The safety boundaries are permanent: no clinical/medical advice, no wet-lab protocols, no dual-use biology, no real patient data, and no claim to be a production untrusted-code sandbox. See Safety and Limitations.

The detailed, honest research path will be maintained in docs/PAPER_PATH.md.
